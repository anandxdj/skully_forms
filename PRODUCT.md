# Skully Forms — Product Context

## Product Purpose
Skully Forms is a Typeform-style conversational form builder with a skeleton / skull mascot theme. Users build beautiful, one-question-at-a-time forms that earn more completions. The brand is playful, slightly spooky, and visually distinctive — not dark and gothic, not childishly cartoonish. Think: a cute skeleton sipping boba tea who also happens to be a serious SaaS product.

## Register
**brand** for Landing, Login, and marketing surfaces.  
**product** for Dashboard, Builder, Form viewer, and Responses.

## Users
- **Primary**: Solo creators, indie hackers, small teams who want beautiful forms without friction.
- **Secondary**: Startups and scaling companies who need branded forms with analytics.
- Both groups are design-aware. They will notice if the UI is generic.

## Brand Tone
Playful but precise. Witty copy, clean execution. The skeleton theme is *consistent and committed* — it is never ironic or self-aware about being weird. Skully is just the mascot; the product is serious.

Anti-tone: horror/edgy, overly cute/childish, generic SaaS blue.

## Color Strategy
**Light mode** — Committed warm cream. Background: `oklch(0.985 0.005 70)` (#FAF8F5). Primary accent: rose-pink `oklch(0.72 0.11 12)`. The scene: a creator at their laptop in a bright café, warm afternoon light, building a beautiful survey.

**Dark mode** — Drenched black. Background: `oklch(0 0 0)` (pure black). Primary accent: vibrant red `oklch(0.55 0.22 25)`. Branded hot-pink `#ff2e8c` used for glow effects. The scene: a developer or power user at night, building forms under focused ambient light.

## Skeleton Illustration System
All key screens have a skeleton character illustration. Characters are: cute, charming, genre-consistent. NOT scary.
- **Hero skeleton** — sitting with boba tea, warm pastel tones (light) / dark edgy style (dark)
- **Developer skeleton** — sitting at laptop/desk (used on login)
- **Gamer skeleton** — headphones, controller (used on success / celebration states)
- **Empty box skeleton** — skeleton with empty box (used for empty states / forgot password)
- **Success skeleton** — celebrating pose

All illustrations have transparent backgrounds. Light and dark variants exist for each.

## Design Anti-references
- Typeform itself (too generic/corporate)
- Google Forms (too utilitarian)
- Formbricks (too developer-tool)
- Generic SaaS dashboards with hero-metric cards and blue gradients

## Strategic Principles
1. Every screen has a skeleton reference — they make the brand memorable and consistent.
2. Illustrations sit *natively on page backgrounds* — no container borders around them.
3. Form builder is the hero product — it should feel fast, focused, and high-fidelity.
4. Stats and analytics matter — responses/completion rates are surfaced prominently.
5. Theme system (slate / cyberpunk / sunset / forest) is a differentiator — showcase it.
